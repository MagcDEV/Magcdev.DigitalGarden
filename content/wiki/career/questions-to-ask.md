# Questions to ask the interviewer

Pick two or three questions per round. Good questions show seniority, and they help you judge the job.

## Engineers (technical rounds)

> [!quote] Say it
> - What does a typical change look like, from ticket to production? How long does it take?
> - What broke in production recently, and what did the team change afterwards?
> - How do you use AI tools in daily work here? What are the rules?
> - Where is the biggest technical debt, and is there time to pay it down?

## Hiring manager

> [!quote] Say it
> - What would make someone in this role clearly successful at six months?
> - What is the team's biggest problem this quarter?
> - How do people grow from Senior to Lead here? Who got promoted recently, and why?
> - How is on-call organised?

## Recruiter (screen): the logistics

In Argentina, the contract type decides the real pay. So ask these questions at the first call.

> [!quote] Say it
> - Is this relación de dependencia, an employer of record, or a contractor role?
> - Is the range gross? Is it in pesos or USD? How is it adjusted?
> - Does the prepaga cover the family?
> - Hybrid: how many days in the office, and where is the office?
> - What are the stages of the process, and what is the timeline?
