      What to watch for

      • Green is the only place anything is decided. Every change to the case goes through the check and the commit, even the ones a worker sends.
      • Nothing outside is called inside the commit. The outbox row is the saved promise. A worker keeps it after the commit.
      • Every arrow carries a key. K1 for the tap, CB-812:PROVISIONAL_CREDIT:v1 for the money, NB-CB-812-1 for the network.
      • Case state and money state move separately. Won is a decision. Paid is a fact. The case closes only when both are true.
      • The version counts commits. A retry with a known key adds no event, so the version does not move.

      The case in one breath

      "Ana opens a dispute with a key; one commit saves the event, the snapshot, a deadline and the promises to call outside; she's told 'opened'. Workers then ask the ledger for credit with a key that names that action, and write back what the ledger said. Evidence is bytes in storage until a checked item is committed. Submission reuses one reference. Deadlines are rows a sweeper can always find. Weeks later the award arrives as an event, money moves again under a new key, and the case closes when decision and settlement agree."
